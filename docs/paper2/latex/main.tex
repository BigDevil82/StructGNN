\begin{abstract}

Shear wall structural optimization for high-rise buildings requires repeated finite element analysis (FEA) and design code checking, making conventional optimization computationally expensive. Although surrogate-assisted methods can reduce evaluation costs, static surrogates often suffer from prediction bias when applied to unseen layouts or evolving local search regions. This study proposes a calibrated surrogate-informed optimization framework (CASIO) that improves optimization efficiency by allocating limited FEA evaluations to high-priority candidates. First, two independently trained layout-conditioned surrogates (FuseGNN) integrate room-adjacency graphs, global layout statistics, and design parameters to estimate candidate feasibility and reinforcement mass prior to FEA. During optimization, FEA-verified samples accumulated from the current building update lightweight online local calibration models, which adjust feasibility probabilities and correct reinforcement prediction residuals without retraining the global networks. The calibrated predictions then guide conservative feasibility screening and cost-oriented candidate ranking. Experiments on 143 engineering shear wall layouts with approximately 715,000 FEA-labeled samples indicate that CASIO reduces true FEA calls by 64.6\% on average across 15 unseen layouts and three search algorithms, while incurring only a 1.19\% mean material cost increase (with a median near zero) across 142 paired feasible runs. These results suggest that online-calibrated surrogates can substantially accelerate shear wall sizing within the implemented checking scope while preserving near-optimal material economy.

\end{abstract}


\begin{highlights}
\item Graph-tabular surrogates capture layout-dependent feasibility and steel demand.
\item FEA feedback enables online local calibration without retraining global models.
\item Local residual calibration reduces reinforcement prediction MAE by over 80\%.
\item Feasibility screening and cost ranking focus FEA on competitive candidates.
\item True FEA calls drop by 64.6\% with only a 1.19\% mean material cost increase.
\end{highlights}

\section*{Data availability statement}
The source code for the CASIO framework, including structural modeling, surrogate model training, and online calibration-assisted optimization, is openly available at \url{https://github.com/BigDevil82/CASIO}. The raw engineering shear wall drawings and derived FEA datasets cannot be publicly released due to proprietary restrictions from the collaborating architectural design institute, but are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable academic request.
