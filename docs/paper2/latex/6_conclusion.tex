\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This study proposed a calibrated surrogate-assisted optimization framework for cross-sectional and material optimization of shear wall structures. A room-graph-based LayoutParamGNN was developed to predict structural feasibility and reinforcement usage, while online local calibration was introduced to correct layout-specific prediction bias using FEA-verified samples accumulated during optimization.

The experimental results lead to the following conclusions:

\begin{enumerate}[(\arabic*)]
\item Layout information improves surrogate accuracy. The room-graph LayoutParamGNN achieved a feasibility PR-AUC of 0.8593 and a reinforcement prediction $R^2$ of 0.924 on the test layouts, outperforming models based only on design parameters or global layout statistics.

\item Online local calibration reduced layout-specific prediction bias. With 100 local FEA samples, the feasibility Brier score decreased from 0.0749 to 0.0437, and the reinforcement MAE decreased from 11.48~t to 2.04~t.

\item The calibrated surrogate-assisted strategy reduced FEA demand with limited cost penalty. Across 15 test layouts and five random seeds, the Screen \& ranking strategy reduced true FEA evaluations by 64.6\% on average, with a 1.19\% average cost increase in paired feasible cases.

\item The method is most effective when feasible candidates exist but FEA evaluations are limited. It can reduce ineffective evaluations, but it cannot overcome an infeasible layout or an overly restrictive design space.
\end{enumerate}

These results show that calibrated surrogate assistance can improve structural optimization efficiency under fixed shear wall layouts. The main limitations are the fixed-layout setting, the dependence of local calibration on representative FEA samples, and the inability of surrogate screening to resolve absent feasible regions. Future work will consider joint layout-section optimization, uncertainty-aware screening, and validation on more complex structural systems and design codes.
