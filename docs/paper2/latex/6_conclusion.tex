\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This study proposed CASIO for FEA-efficient section and material design of shear wall structures. By combining FuseGNN with online local calibration and feasibility-aware cost ranking, CASIO concentrates limited structural evaluations on competitive candidate designs. Based on experiments across 143 real-world shear wall layouts, the main conclusions are summarized as follows:

\begin{enumerate}[(1)]
\item Incorporating room-adjacency graphs and global layout statistics enables FuseGNN to capture layout-dependent structural behavior, achieving a feasibility PR-AUC of 0.8593 and a reinforcement prediction $R^2$ of 0.924 on unseen layouts, which substantially outperforms parameter-only baselines.

\item Adapting frozen global predictions using FEA-verified samples from the current building effectively corrects layout-specific systematic bias. In controlled local evaluations with 100 samples per layout, residual calibration reduces the feasibility Brier score from 0.0749 to 0.0437 and lowers reinforcement MAE by 82.2\% (from 11.48~t to 2.04~t) while preserving high feasible recall.

\item Across 15 unseen test layouts and three search algorithms (GA, PSO, and random search), the combined screening-and-ranking strategy reduces true FEA calls by an average of 64.6\% while accelerating first-feasible discovery, incurring a mean material cost increase of only 1.19\% (with a median near 0\%) across 142 paired feasible runs.

\item Reinforcement prediction and feasibility screening play complementary roles across layouts of varying difficulty. Reinforcement prediction steers the search away from costly designs, while feasibility screening prevents optimizers from being trapped in low-cost infeasible regions on challenging layouts.
\end{enumerate}

This work focuses on section and material sizing under a prescribed shear wall layout, where local calibration relies on search-selected samples and rejected candidates are not evaluated by FEA. Future work will extend this framework to uncertainty-aware adaptive screening, matched-budget ablation analyses, and joint optimization of shear wall layout topology and member sizing.
