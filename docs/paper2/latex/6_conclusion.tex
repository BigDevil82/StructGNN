\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This study proposed CASINO, a calibrated surrogate-informed optimization framework, for cross-sectional and material optimization of shear wall structures. A layout-conditioned surrogate was developed to predict structural feasibility and reinforcement usage from room-level graph representations, while online local calibration was introduced to correct layout-specific prediction bias using FEA-verified samples accumulated during optimization.

The experimental results show that CASINO can improve structural optimization efficiency under fixed shear wall layouts, leading to the following conclusions:

\begin{enumerate}[(\arabic*)]
\item Layout information improves surrogate accuracy. The proposed FuseGNN achieved a feasibility PR-AUC of 0.8593 and a reinforcement prediction $R^2$ of 0.924 on the test layouts, outperforming models based only on design parameters or global layout statistics.

\item Online local calibration can reduce layout-specific prediction bias. With 100 local FEA samples, the feasibility Brier score decreased from 0.0749 to 0.0437, and the reinforcement MAE decreased from 11.48~t to 2.04~t.

\item The calibrated surrogate-informed strategy can reduce FEA demand with limited cost penalty. Across 15 test layouts and five random seeds, the Screen \& ranking strategy reduced true FEA evaluations by 64.6\% on average, with a 1.19\% average cost increase in paired feasible cases.

\item The method is most effective when feasible candidates exist within the design space but evaluating the full candidate population by FEA is computationally prohibitive under the available budget.
\end{enumerate}

These findings demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed framework while also highlighting several directions for further development. The main limitations are the fixed-layout setting, the dependence of local calibration on representative FEA samples, and the inability of surrogate screening to resolve absent feasible regions. Future work will consider joint layout-section optimization, uncertainty-aware screening, and validation on more complex structural systems and design codes.
