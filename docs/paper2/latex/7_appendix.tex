\section{Definition of Decision and Optimization Metrics}
\label{app:evaluation_metrics}

Standard classification, regression, probability calibration (Brier score~\cite{Brier1950} and ECE~\cite{Naeini2015}), and rank-correlation metrics (Spearman's $\rho_s$~\cite{Spearman1904}) follow their conventional definitions. This appendix defines the task-specific screening, ranking, and optimization metrics used in this study.

For candidate screening with threshold $\tau$ and true binary feasibility labels $y_i \in \{0,1\}$, let $\hat{p}_{f,i}$ denote the predicted or calibrated feasibility score. Feasibility recall ($R_{\mathrm{fea}}$), pre-FEA reject rate ($R_{\mathrm{rej}}$), and false-reject count ($N_{\mathrm{fr}}$) are defined as
\begin{equation}
R_{\mathrm{fea}} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbb{I}(y_i=1)\mathbb{I}(\hat{p}_{f,i}\geq \tau)}{\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbb{I}(y_i=1)}, \qquad
R_{\mathrm{rej}} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbb{I}(\hat{p}_{f,i}<\tau)}{N}, \qquad
N_{\mathrm{fr}} = \sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbb{I}(y_i=1)\mathbb{I}(\hat{p}_{f,i}<\tau),
\label{eq:app_screening_metrics}
\end{equation}
where $\mathbb{I}(\cdot)$ is the indicator function. For reinforcement ranking, pairwise ranking accuracy ($A_{\mathrm{rank}}$) and top-$k$ recall ($R_{\mathrm{top}\text{-}k}$) are defined as
\begin{equation}
A_{\mathrm{rank}} = \frac{\sum_{i<j}\mathbb{I}\!\left[(m_{s,i}-m_{s,j})(\hat{m}_{s,i}-\hat{m}_{s,j}) > 0\right]}{\binom{N}{2}}, \qquad
R_{\mathrm{top}\text{-}k} = \frac{|\mathcal{T}_k \cap \hat{\mathcal{T}}_k|}{|\mathcal{T}_k|},
\label{eq:app_ranking_metrics}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{T}_k$ and $\hat{\mathcal{T}}_k$ denote the sets of candidates with the lowest $k$ true and predicted reinforcement masses, respectively.

For optimization benchmarking, the true FEA call count ($N_{\mathrm{FEA}}$), FEA reduction ratio ($R_{\mathrm{FEA}}$), feasible-solution discovery rate ($R_{\mathrm{succ}}$), paired objective cost ratio ($R_{\mathrm{obj}}$), and FEA calls to first feasible design ($N_{\mathrm{first}}$) are given by
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{FEA}} &= \sum_{t}N_{\mathrm{new},t} + N_{\mathrm{final}}, &\qquad
R_{\mathrm{FEA}} &= 1 - \frac{N_{\mathrm{FEA}}^{\mathrm{sur}}}{N_{\mathrm{FEA}}^{\mathrm{full}}}, &\qquad
R_{\mathrm{succ}} &= \frac{N_{\mathrm{runs}}^{\mathrm{feasible}}}{N_{\mathrm{runs}}}, \\
R_{\mathrm{obj}} &= \frac{C_{\mathrm{best}}^{\mathrm{sur}}}{C_{\mathrm{best}}^{\mathrm{full}}}, &\qquad
N_{\mathrm{first}} &= \min\left\{n \mid \exists i \leq n,\ y_{f,i}=1\right\},
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:app_optimization_metrics}
\end{equation}
where $N_{\mathrm{new},t}$ counts uncached structural evaluations in iteration $t$, $N_{\mathrm{final}} \in \{0,1\}$ accounts for final verification of an unevaluated provisional solution, and superscripts $\mathrm{sur}$ and $\mathrm{full}$ denote surrogate-informed and full-FEA runs under identical layouts and random seeds.

For the room-graph summaries, node and directed-edge counts are normalized by 64 and 256, respectively, and room areas are normalized by the squared layout scale. The tabular encoder processes 49 standardized numerical features (8 design inputs and 41 layout statistics) concatenated with four 4-dimensional categorical embeddings. With graph hidden width $H$, the fused representation concatenates mean- and max-pooled node embeddings ($2H$), mean-pooled edge embeddings ($H/2$), the tabular embedding ($H$), and the seven graph summaries, yielding a vector of dimension $3.5H+7$ (903 for $\Phi_f$ and 455 for $\Phi_s$) passed to a two-layer MLP head with rectified linear unit activation (ReLU) and dropout.

\subsection{Evaluated Fitness and Code-Checking Scope}

For an evaluated candidate with valid material quantities, the scalar fitness returned to the optimizer is
\begin{equation}
F(x)=
\begin{cases}
C(x), & y_f(x)=1,\\
P_{\mathrm{inf}}+\lambda_v V(x)+\lambda_c C(x), & y_f(x)=0,
\end{cases}
\label{eq:true_fitness}
\end{equation}
where $V(x)$ sums normalized upper-bound violations $\max\{0,(a-\ell)/\ell\}$, lower-bound violations $\max\{0,(\ell-a)/\ell\}$, and binary failed-check indicators.

The automated structural check enforces a torsional response ratio limit of 1.5, an interstory drift limit of $1/1000$, adjacent- and three-story-average stiffness ratio lower limits of 0.7 and 0.8, a torsional-to-translational period ratio limit of 0.9, and minimum shear-to-weight ratios of 0.008, 0.016, 0.024, and 0.032 for seismic intensities 6, 7, 7.5, and 8, respectively, together with code-specified wall axial compression and wall/beam shear-compression checks~\cite{GB500112010,GB500102010}.
