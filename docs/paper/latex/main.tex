\begin{abstract}
Shear wall layout design for high-rise buildings remains labor-intensive in preliminary design. Existing generative AI methods are commonly formulated as image-to-image translation or graph learning on basic geometric components, but these formulations are often inefficient and insufficient for capturing room-level spatial semantics. This paper proposes RC-GNN, a room-level conditional graph neural network for preliminary shear wall layout generation. By representing rooms as nodes and room adjacencies as edges, RC-GNN links architectural space organization with boundary-feasible wall placement while reducing graph complexity. Conditional modulation and a dual-stream training strategy are further introduced to support \textcolor{red}{discrete} density-controlled generation under limited paired engineering data. Experiments on 143 residential floor plans show that RC-GNN improves layout prediction over a component-based GNN baseline. Ablation analysis and finite-element case studies indicate that the proposed method enhances controllability and provides structurally plausible preliminary layouts.
\end{abstract}
