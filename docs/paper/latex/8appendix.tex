\section{Additional qualitative results on test cases}
\label{app:additional_qualitative}

\textcolor{red}{This appendix presents additional qualitative comparisons between the generated shear wall layouts and the corresponding engineer-designed layouts. The cases are selected from the test folds and cover low-, medium-, and high-density conditions. The results further illustrate the model's ability to generate boundary-aligned wall layouts under different floor-plan configurations. Some less satisfactory cases are also included to show typical errors, such as missing local wall segments, over-prediction near dense boundary regions, and reduced accuracy in layouts with more complex room partitioning.}
